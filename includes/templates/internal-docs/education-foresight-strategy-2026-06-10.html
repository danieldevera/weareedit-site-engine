1How to read this

Google Trends "interest" is 0–100, relative to the peak day within each individual query. Values are NOT comparable across verticals — each vertical has its own 0-100 scale. Within a vertical, the relative order tells you what Portugal is searching for.

Note on the downward trends: Most terms show declining interest from March → June. This is partly seasonal (Portuguese vacation cycle starts in May; September enrolments drop search activity until late August) and partly an artifact of how Google Trends normalizes daily values. Treat the relative ranking within each vertical as the high-confidence signal; treat absolute trend slopes as directional only.

6PT 12-month deep — seasonality cycle discovered

Stretching the window to 52 weeks reveals the real Portuguese search rhythm. The June dip we saw on 90-day data is NOT a real decline — it's the bottom of an annual cycle that snaps back hard in Sep-Nov and again Feb-Mar.

Top 5 anchor terms — weekly, 12 months

Seasonality pulse — the two peaks

🍂 Surge #1 — Sep–Nov (post-summer "back to upskilling")

Sharp lift across figma · python · project management · agile · marketing digital. Peaks in October/November weekly. This is your Setembro 2026 cohort gold zone. EARLY15 closing 30 Jun captures the *intent* before the summer dip; the next big buying window opens Sep 2025-style starting Sep 8.

❄️ Surge #2 — Feb–Mar (post-Christmas "new year resolution")

The biggest annual peaks for python · figma · react · chatgpt · agile · project management hit weeks 6–11 of the year. Portugal makes its biggest learning decisions in Feb-March, NOT September. This is the window for Maio 2026-style cohorts + workshop bookings.

🌴 The summer floor — Jun–Aug

Universal dip across all terms. June 23–Aug 31. Don't burn budget on cold acquisition in this window. Use it for: editorial (newsletter), brand (LinkedIn personal share), retargeting warm cohorts who saw EARLY15 — not net-new acquisition.

⚠ Your hypothesis ("PT 6-12 months behind") — partially validated, partially not

For mainstream consumer AI (ChatGPT): NO, PT is NOT 6-12 months behind. PT peaked 3 months ago, while Germany is peaking right now. PT is squarely in the middle of the EU adoption curve — slightly behind UK/NL/ES but ahead of DE. This means the AI-adoption-lag thesis isn't a reliable predictor for consumer-grade tools.

For advanced professional tooling (AI agents · Claude Code · MCP · Figma+AI): YES, PT lags UK by likely 6-12 months. See the rising-queries section below — UK is searching heavily for "claude code", "ai agents", "context engineering", "figma mcp" — none of which register in PT data yet. This is where PT genuinely trails, and where EDIT.'s product pipeline should anchor.

Practical translation: Don't try to forecast PT mainstream adoption from UK mainstream data (the gap is short or non-existent). DO forecast PT professional/specialist adoption from UK rising-queries (real 6-12mo signal).

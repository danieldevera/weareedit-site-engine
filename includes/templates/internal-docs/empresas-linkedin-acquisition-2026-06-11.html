Brevo attribution

Every deal auto-created in Brevo via the Lead Gen Form is tagged with SOURCE_CHANNEL = linkedin_ads + UTM_CAMPAIGN = empresas_2026_h2. This lets us calculate true cost-per-deal (not just cost-per-lead).

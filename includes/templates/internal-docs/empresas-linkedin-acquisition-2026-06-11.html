🎯 Strategic implication — the actual landing page H1

PT corporate buyers anchor on the TOOL NAME (ChatGPT) + regulatory check (DGERT) + fiscal credit (SIFIDE). Abstract concepts ("formação corporativa", "upskilling", "IA para empresas", "formação digital para empresas") have zero verbatim organic demand — though the underlying intent IS captured by adjacent terms (notably Cheque Formação up to Jun 30, after which we lose that proxy signal entirely). The category term "academia digital" has high volume but fragmented intent across banks/telcos — not a winnable anchor.

The optimal landing page H1 compounds the 3 anchors EDIT. can actually win + that survive Jun 30:

"Formação ChatGPT para Empresas. DGERT-certificada. SIFIDE-elegível."

Why this works: "formação ChatGPT" + "ChatGPT empresas" are 55-65 avg with multiple 90+ peaks — high volume + clear training-buyer intent + uncontested by big PT brands. DGERT (25 avg) provides the regulatory trust signal. SIFIDE (11 avg, niche but consistent) replaces Cheque Formação as the surviving financial-trigger anchor for CFOs.

Drop from positioning: "Cheque Formação" (sunsetting Jun 30, 2026 — don't anchor on a dying program), "Academia Digital" (Santander/BPI/NOS/EDP/IEFP compete for the head term), "formação corporativa" (zero PT search volume), "upskilling/reskilling" (anglicisms with no PT organic pull), "IA para empresas" (PT buyers search the tool, not the concept), "POPH" (already defunct).

This doubles the strength of Angle A (reframed from generic "AI changed your team" → specific "ChatGPT changed how your team works"). Angle C (Financial / CFO hook) now rests on DGERT + SIFIDE + POPH-successor (TBD). Angle B (social proof) is brand-driven and works regardless of search vocabulary.

⚠ Note on the "formação digital para empresas" zero-volume reading: Google Trends has a detection threshold (~1-5 searches/week minimum) below which terms show null even when there ARE real searches. The verbatim phrase isn't dominant, but the underlying buyer intent IS real — it was being carried in the Trends data by adjacent terms (Cheque Formação as program brand, formação ChatGPT, DGERT). With Cheque Formação sunsetting, that proxy signal disappears and the brief over-weighted it. Anchor the campaign on ChatGPT-specific intent + DGERT + SIFIDE instead.

📅 Seasonal timing — September WAS the surge (now uncertain post-Cheque Formação)

In 2025, both DGERT (52 → 62) and Cheque Formação (14 → 100) spiked sharply in week 37 (Sep 14) — coincided with the annual Cheque Formação + Digital program announcement. With Cheque Formação sunsetting Jun 30, 2026, the September surge is unlikely to repeat at the same magnitude in 2026. DGERT's organic baseline will likely persist; the financial-trigger half disappears unless a successor program is announced.

Best-guess buying-window structure (to validate after Jun 30):

  • Sep-Oct: Likely still the decision window (academic + fiscal cycle aligned), but at lower amplitude than 2025.
  • Nov-Dec: Procurement + contracts. Q1 launches negotiated.
  • Jun-Aug: Off-cycle. Brand awareness OK, conversion campaigns waste budget.

Practical translation: launching the LinkedIn campaign in June is still correct — it builds awareness + retargeting pool. But scale plan must adapt — monitor DGERT + ChatGPT empresas trends through August, don't pre-commit Sep-Oct spending until we see the post-Cheque-Formação search dynamics. Reserve 40% (not 60%) of annual budget for Aug 25 → Oct 15 until 2026 data confirms a surge.
